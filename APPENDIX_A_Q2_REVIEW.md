### 1. Documentation quality
We audited 1,705 notes in Q2 (1 Apr – 30 Jun). The pass rate came in at 79.9%, against our 90% target - the third consecutive quarter below target and the largest gap yet. Average composite score was 0.9055.

The rubric was revised mid-quarter (v1 → v2) to put more weight on accuracy and completeness. Scoring continuity was maintained throughout; the audit platform team confirmed the migration was clean.

Decision A - proposed. Fund a Q3 remediation program: mandatory retraining for all MDS pods, audit coverage raised from ~27% to 40% of notes, and one additional QA headcount. Estimated cost $410K annualised.
### 2. Delivery SLA
Delivery SLA breach rate was 12.8%, median delivery 18.5 minutes. Targets were tightened on 15 May as planned; this analysis uses our current SLA targets throughout for consistency.
### 3. Escalations
149 escalations remain open, with average time to first response of 49.5 minutes - comfortably inside our 60-minute commitment. Every escalation raised in the quarter received a response.
### 4. Volume stability
The daily volume alert flagged 28 of 91 days as anomalous (>30% below trailing 14-day average). This is a sharp deterioration in demand stability and warrants a capacity review with Workforce Planning.
### 5. MDS performance
Leaderboard ranked by average composite score across notes handled. Top performer this quarter: Marchetti, Ana (163 notes, 0.9255). Bottom five are listed in the attachment.

Decision B - proposed. Quarterly recognition award to the top of the leaderboard. Bottom five placed on a documented 60-day coaching plan, HR-tracked.
