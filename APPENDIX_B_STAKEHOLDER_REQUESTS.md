# APPENDIX B - Stakeholder requests for the Triage Workbench

Verbatim excerpts. These arrived separately, over two weeks, in three different channels. Nobody has reconciled them. That is part of your job.


R-01 · Ops Director - Slack DM

Honestly the thing I want most is to see the whole queue on one screen. No scrolling, no pagination, no "next 50". I want to look at it and know where we stand. And when I've decided on a case I want to click the row and have it be done - one click, straight from the table. I don't want to hunt for a Save button.

R-02 · QA Lead - email

The reviewer cannot make a defensible call without the full note text in front of them and all seven sub-scores visible at the same time. Nothing hidden behind a click, nothing in a tooltip. I've watched people get this wrong when they're guessing from a truncated preview.

Separately: anything that changes a clinician-facing record needs a deliberate second confirmation. We've had accidental dispositions before and it created a real mess with a physician group.

R-03 · Head of Product, LT - meeting notes

Monday morning queues run 2,500–3,000 cases after the weekend backlog. Design for that, not for the 40 cases you'll see when you're testing on a Wednesday.

R-04 · Compliance - ticket comment

Every state change must be attributable to a named user, timestamped, and reversible. If we can't answer "who changed this and when" for any individual case, we can't ship it.

R-05 · Ops Director - follow-up Slack DM, four days later

One more thing, the leads are fast and they hate the mouse. If they could just keep their hands on the keyboard and blow through the queue that'd be the single biggest win.

R-06 · Head of Product, LT - Slack
